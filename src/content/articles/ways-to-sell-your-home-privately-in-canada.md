# Ways to sell your home privately in Canada, compared

**Short answer:** there are three main ways to sell your home without a full-service agent in Canada. **For-sale-by-owner (FSBO) sites** such as OwnersMarket, PropertyGuys and ComFree advertise your home for a fee instead of a commission. **Flat-fee MLS services** such as Bōde, ComFree's MLS packages and other flat-fee brokerages put your home on the MLS® and REALTOR.ca, where agents and their buyers look. **Free classifieds** such as Kijiji and Facebook Marketplace cost little but give you no support. Whichever you pick, you still need a real estate lawyer (or a notary in BC) to handle the sale.

## 2. For-sale-by-owner sites

A for-sale-by-owner site advertises your home to buyers who are looking for private sales. You set the price, show the home and talk with buyers; your lawyer handles the paperwork once you agree on a deal.

- **OwnersMarket** charges $24.99 a month per active listing, with no commission and nothing to pay when it sells. Every listing is reviewed before it goes live. It's an advertising site, not a brokerage, so it doesn't put your home on the MLS®. It's open in every province and territory except Québec.
- **PropertyGuys** works through local franchises and sells flat-fee packages. Its site says packages can be customized and that it offers a free consultation and quote.
- **ComFree** charges a one-time upfront fee. Its site says all packages include MLS® and REALTOR.ca exposure, which puts it partly in the next group too.
- **ForSaleByOwner.ca** offers packages with and without a local board MLS® listing.

**Good to know:** without the MLS®, your home won't show up on REALTOR.ca, where many buyers and agents search. You'll lean more on your own advertising: a sign on the lawn, sharing the listing, and the for-sale-by-owner site itself. See [how to list your house for sale by owner](/guides/how-to-list-your-house-for-sale-by-owner).

## 3. Flat-fee MLS® services

A flat-fee MLS® service is a licensed brokerage that puts your home on the MLS® for a set fee, so it appears on REALTOR.ca. Most of the selling is still up to you.

- **Bōde** publishes two plans: $949 + GST paid upfront (a 180-day listing), or 1% + GST of the sale price, capped at $10,000, paid only if the home sells (a 90-day listing). It's a brokerage in Alberta, British Columbia, Ontario and Saskatchewan.
- **ComFree** and **ForSaleByOwner.ca** also sell packages that include an MLS® listing.

**Good to know:** most buyers who find you through the MLS® have an agent, and that agent will usually expect to be paid. Budget for a buyer's agent commission, and agree on it in writing before any offer. See [selling privately when the buyer has an agent](/guides/selling-privately-when-buyer-has-an-agent).

## 4. Free and low-cost classifieds

**Kijiji** and **Facebook Marketplace** reach a lot of people at little or no cost. They don't check listings closely, and you'll get more time-wasters and scam messages than on a dedicated site. Many sellers use them as an extra place to advertise, alongside a for-sale-by-owner listing, rather than on their own.

## 5. How to choose

1. **How much do you want to spend?** If the lowest cost matters most, a for-sale-by-owner site plus free classifieds costs the least.
2. **Do you need agents' buyers?** If homes like yours mostly sell through agents in your area, a flat-fee MLS® listing gets you in front of them. Budget for the buyer's agent.
3. **How much help do you want?** A local franchise or a brokerage gives you more hands-on help, for a higher fee.
4. **Can you show the home yourself?** Every option here except a full-service agent expects you to do the showings and talk with buyers.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best website to sell my house by owner in Canada?**
It depends on what you need. If you want the lowest cost and to deal with buyers directly, a for-sale-by-owner site like OwnersMarket ($24.99 a month, no commission) fits. If you want your home on REALTOR.ca, choose a flat-fee MLS® service and budget for a buyer's agent.

**Can I list my house on REALTOR.ca without a realtor?**
Not directly. Only licensed brokerages can put homes on the MLS® and REALTOR.ca. A flat-fee MLS® service is a brokerage that does it for a set fee.

**Is it free to sell a house on Kijiji or Facebook Marketplace?**
Posting costs little or nothing, but check each site's current rules and fees. You'll still pay your lawyer, as with any sale.

**Do I still need a lawyer if I sell privately?**
Yes. A real estate lawyer (or, in BC, a lawyer or notary) handles the offer paperwork, the deposit, the title transfer and paying off your mortgage. See [what a real estate lawyer does when you sell](/guides/what-a-real-estate-lawyer-does-when-you-sell).
